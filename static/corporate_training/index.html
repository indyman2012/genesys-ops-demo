Corporate Training Queue Roster

Upload an Excel list of agent names (one column: Name, Agent, or Email). Changes run against Genesys using server credentials after you sign in.

1 — Start training (pause production queues)

Confirms each person is on at least one Corporate Training queue. For any other queue where they are joined, sets joined=false so they receive training calls only.

2 — Return from training

Removes each person from every corporate training queue, then sets joined=true on their production queues (non-training memberships).

Hosting & sign-in

Open this page from your GenesysCloudToolkit ops site at /corporate-training/ (not as a local file).

Sign in with a Genesys supervisor/admin account when prompted. Preview runs are safe; Apply performs live updates.
