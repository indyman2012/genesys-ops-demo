Troubleshooting

Sign in with Genesys is the recommended path. It does not require the bookmarklet or being on a specific tab.

If sign-in fails, confirm the OAuth redirect URI is registered in Genesys Admin for this site.

If no WebRTC phone appears, ask IT to confirm you have a phone named {Your Name} Web assigned in Genesys.

If a site is missing from the list, you may not have telephony permissions for that site — contact your supervisor or Genesys admin.
